Identity & Conflict Rules

  • Primary Key: Composite key (source_type, source_id) everywhere.
  • Display Labels: record_ref (B01, C01, M01) is purely an envelope label.
  • Precedence Rule: Higher batch revision wins (rev 2 overwrites rev 1). Equal or older batches never overwrite.
  • Same-Revision Diff: Diff on same revision produces a logged warning, not an overwrite.

Known Limitations

  • All 44 interactions fall on a single local date (21 August 2026 IST). Multi-day trending requires larger batches.
  • No pipeline revenue totals or win probabilities are fabricated without CRM contract feeds.
  • Pre-generated analyses are point-in-time snapshots; updated transcripts raise a stale flag.
